## Photography Direction — Read This First

Think **Urban Company** / **Hinge-era lifestyle** photography. Every image
should look like it was shot by a commercial photographer on a proper DSLR with
a 35mm or 50mm prime lens — not AI-generated, not stock. The vibe is:

> "A calm, confident professional doing skilled work inside a bright, modern
> home. Candid. Honest. Premium."

### The Look
- **Interiors:** Modern urban apartment. Clean white or warm-greige walls.
  Minimal décor. Could be Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi — any Indian metro. No
  clichés (no ceiling fans in frame, no grilled windows, no clay tiles, no
  calendar on the wall).
- **Light:** Abundant, soft, directional natural window light from one side.
  Slightly warm cast (~4500K). No fill flash. Gentle shadows that give depth.
  Think overcast-bright, not golden hour.
- **Color grade:** Muted, film-like. Slightly desaturated highlights. Teal
  shadows complement the brand palette (`#00B8C4` / `#0E1F3D`). Comparable to
  Lightroom's "Clean Fade" preset.
- **Framing:** Loose medium or medium-close. Some breathing room. Rule of
  thirds. Slight angle, never flat-on. Depth of field: f/2.8–f/4 so background
  is soft but the work surface is sharp.
- **People:** South Asian professionals — 25–38 years old. Trim, well-groomed,
  calm focused expression. Looking at their work, not the camera. Realistic
  skin tones, no over-smoothed AI skin.
- **Uniforms:** Crisp teal (`#00B8C4`) or dark navy (`#0E1F3D`) polo or
  work shirt. Tucked or half-tucked. No logos, no brand names visible on any
  gear or clothing.

### Absolute Rules (violating any = redo)
1. **Zero text, numbers, logos, watermarks** in any image. The app renders
   text on top.
2. **Zero stock-photo body language:** no thumbs-up, no crossed arms, no
   camera-facing smile, no "ta-da" pose.
3. **Zero AI tells:** check hands carefully — correct finger count, natural
   grip. Tool shapes must be accurate. No duplicate or smeared elements.
4. **Zero orange/red/yellow uniforms** — they clash with the brand palette.
5. **The subject is the work, not the person.** Hands and tools should be
   in sharp focus or compositionally dominant.

## Why filenames are locked

`packages/mocks/src/fixtures/catalog.ts` derives image paths automatically:

```ts
imageUrls: [`/mock/services/${name.toLowerCase().replace(/[^a-z0-9]+/g, "-")}.jpg`]
```

Example: `"AC service & repair"` → `/mock/services/ac-service-repair.jpg`.
A mismatched filename is a broken image.
